                        Deployment Requirements
▪ Those are Requirements that are usually set upon negotiation and iterative process between the
  two parties (only when the S/C is in lead or in dedicated configuration, otherwise these conditions
  are just set by the LSP)
▪ One central aspect to be set here is the deployment strategy:
       ▪   For instance, consider a launch with 2 main passengers
       ▪   The customers communicate to the LSP the direction at which they would like to be deployed and the LSP
           assess if it is possible to guarantee the needs of both passengers or not (see manoeuvres for deployment
           from Vega-C AVUM on next slide)

             • Be deployed in a direction perpendicular to the Sun
             • Be deployed in a nadir-pointing direction
             • Be deployed in a direction such that the separation

       ▪   The customers communicate to the LSP the maximum tip-off rate at which they would like to be deployed
           and the LSP assess if it is possible to guarantee the needs of both passengers or not

       ▪   A very important concern in this negotiations of the Deployment Conditions is the Assessment of the

    LSP Selection Decision Making Tips
▪   First, be aware that the Launch represents a significant cost of the project, important decision and subjected to
    a lot of pressure from the different players.
▪   Launch selection is (theoretically) not subjected to Geo-Return.
▪   Several aspects to be considered when taking this decision:
        ▪ Geopolitical factors
        ▪ Declared Launch availability periods
        ▪ Feasibility: risk assessment by means of FMECA
        ▪ Cost
        ▪ Technical assessment (requirements)

       Assign a score and a weight to each of the factors considered and merge them on a global score

▪   Different LSPs may require different environmental requirements -> How do we start designing the S/C and
    defining requirements if the LSP has not been decided yet?
        ▪ One possible solution: Consider the Envelope of different LSPs (f.e. in mechanical levels)

▪   If a requirement towards a LSP is not met, try to communicate them as study if it would be feasible or not to
    process a RFD (Request for Deviation) or RFW (Request For Waiver)
         ▪ If this is not possible at all, this LSP shall be ruled out as soon as possible
▪   In the actual market several new micro-launchers are about to produce big changes: from an institutional
    paradigm to a commercial paradigm, and this offers new capabilities specially for small/medium size satellites
         ▪ However, keep always in mind that building a Rocket from scratch is not easy: their technological (and
            financial risk is quite high nowadays)
         ▪ Are Micro launchers companies accomplishing the launch dates they promised?
         ▪ If you are about to select one of this companies the insight of their development program shall be very
            high. Are they offering you all the info you need to take a decision?
